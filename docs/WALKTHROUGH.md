# Player Walkthrough — How to Play Desi Guess

This guide walks you through a complete **Local Pass-and-Play** match of **Desi Guess – Local Edition** from opening the app to viewing the final match results.

---

## 1. Opening the Application

- Launch **Desi Guess**. After the animated **Splash Screen**, first-time players see a brief 3-slide **Onboarding** overview of the 450+ Desi cards, five-heart deduction rules, and offline Pass-and-Play privacy handoffs.
- Tap **Next** through the slides (or **Skip** at the top right) to reach **Player Setup**.
- On subsequent launches, the app takes you straight to the **Home Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Setting Up Your Local Profile

- On **Player Setup**, choose an avatar preset and enter your player nickname, then tap **Enter DESI GUESS**.
- Your profile, stats, achievements, and match history are stored locally on your device—no account or internet connection is required.

---

## 3. Starting a Local Game

From the **Home Dashboard**, you can start a match in three ways:

- Tap the saffron **PLAY TOGETHER** button in the hero card (or the **Play** tab in the bottom bar) to open **Pass & Play Setup**.
- Tap any Featured Category tile (**WHO**, **WHAT**, or **WHERE**) to immediately deal a randomized 10-card board using your profile name and `Player 2`.
- Tap **CUSTOM** (or the **Boards** tab) to build and play your own private custom deck.

*(If you closed the app mid-match, a **Resume Local Match** banner appears at the top of the Home Dashboard so you can jump right back in.)*

---

## 4. Setting Up Players

- On **Pass & Play Setup**, enter names for **Player 1 Name** and **Player 2 Name**.
- Tap **Select Category & Play Local** (or **Start Local Match Now** if you opened setup from a specific pack).

---

## 5. Choosing the Game Category

- On **Choose WHO, WHAT or WHERE**, select one of the three primary categories:
  - **WHO** — 250 cards across 10 themes (Bollywood, Cricket, Singers, Politicians, Sports, TV, Influencers, Cartoons, Memes)
  - **WHAT** — 128 cards across 8 themes (Food, Brands, Vehicles, Movies, Household Objects, Technology, Snacks, Products)
  - **WHERE** — 72 cards across 6 themes (Cities, Tourist Attractions, States, Monuments, Colleges, Destinations)
- Every choice automatically mixes cards across all subcategories of that category.

---

## 6. Understanding the 10-Card Board

- On **Board Preview**, inspect the **10 freshly randomized cards** that both players will share for this match.
- Want a different mix? Tap **Shuffle New 10 Cards** to generate a brand-new set.
- When both players are happy with the board, tap **Start Match** (or **Lock Board & Deal Secrets**).

---

## 7. Selecting & Protecting Secret Cards

- For randomized 10-card matches, Desi Guess automatically assigns each player a **distinct secret card** from the board.
- For custom boards, each player manually taps a card on the **Secret Card Selection** grid and taps **Lock Secret Card**.
- Player 1 views/locks their secret card first, then the screen switches to the **Privacy Handoff** cover so Player 2 can privately view/lock theirs without seeing Player 1's card.

---

## 8. Passing the Device Between Players

- Before every player's turn, the **Privacy Handoff** screen hides the game board and displays whose turn is next (e.g., **Pass the Phone to Meera**).
- Hand the phone to the named player. Once only they can see the screen, they tap **I Am Ready (`<Player Name>`)** to reveal their private board.

---

## 9. Asking Questions & Eliminating Cards

- On the **Main Game Board**, you see:
  - Top header with both players' names and **5-heart indicators** (`HeartsIndicator`).
  - Status bar showing how many opponent candidate cards remain (`Remaining`), a **Restore All** option, and a **Hold to Peek Secret** pill (press and hold to check your own secret card).
  - The 10-card grid, where your own secret card is badged **`MY CARD`** (it cannot be eliminated and is excluded from your guess list).
- Ask **unlimited Yes/No questions out loud** to your opponent. Normal questions never cost hearts!
- Tap any card to **eliminate** (dim) it as you rule it out; tap again anytime to **restore** it.
- *(Optional)* Tap the **note icon** in the bottom bar to log a question & Yes/No answer into **Question History** (viewable via the history icon in the top bar).

---

## 10. Making a Final Guess

- When you think you know your opponent's secret card, tap **Final Guess** in the bottom bar.
- In the Final Guess sheet, select your suspect from the remaining opponent candidate cards (your own `MY CARD` is automatically excluded).
- Tap **Lock Final Guess: `<Card Name>`** to submit.

---

## 11. What Happens After an Incorrect Guess & Automatic Turn Switching

- If your guess is **wrong**:
  1. **1 heart is immediately deducted** from your heart counter.
  2. A floating message appears:
     > `"Incorrect Guess! 1 Heart Lost. It's <NextPlayer>'s Turn."`
  3. Your turn **ends automatically** and transitions straight to the **Privacy Handoff** screen for the next active player.
- There is **no manual "End Turn" button**—turn switching happens automatically after an incorrect final guess, and the next player taps **I Am Ready** to take their normal turn.

---

## 12. Heart Deductions & Elimination

- Each player starts with **5 hearts**.
- Every incorrect final guess removes **1 heart**.
- If a player loses their **5th and final heart (`0` hearts left)**, they are immediately eliminated—instead of switching turns, the game ends right away and declares the surviving player the winner.

---

## 13. How to Win the Game

You win a match of Desi Guess by either:
- **Correctly guessing your opponent's secret card** with a Final Guess (instant victory!), or
- **Outlasting your opponent** when their incorrect guesses reduce them to `0` hearts.

---

## 14. Understanding the Final Results Screen

When the match ends, the **Match Results** screen displays:
- The **winner announcement** and win reason (e.g., *Guessed "Delhi" correctly!* or *ran out of hearts!*).
- **Both players' secret cards revealed** side by side.
- Match statistics: Board Pack, Questions Asked, Wrong Guesses, Remaining Hearts, and Match Duration.
- Action buttons to launch a **Rematch** (deals a fresh randomized 10-card board!) or return **Back to Home**.

---

## 15. Tips for Playing with Family & Friends

- **Start broad with Yes/No questions**: Split the 10-card board in half early (e.g., *"Is this person a sportsperson?"*, *"Is this a sweet dish?"*, *"Is this place in North India?"*).
- **Use long-press for hints**: Long-press any card on the board to view its description and attributes if a player isn't sure who or what a card is.
- **Don't rush Final Guesses**: Asking verbal questions is free and unlimited—only tap **Final Guess** when you have narrowed the board down to 1–2 strong candidates.
- **Try Hinglish mode**: Open **Settings** (gear icon on Home) to switch the UI between **EN** and **Hinglish**.
- **Make a Custom Board**: Create a family or college-friends deck in **Custom Boards** (6+ cards) for inside jokes and personalized rounds!
