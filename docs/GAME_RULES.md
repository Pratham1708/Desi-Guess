# Game Rules — Desi Guess (Local Edition)

Desi Guess is a two-player deduction game played face-to-face on a single phone in **Local Pass-and-Play** mode.

---

## 1. Player Setup

- Enter names for **Player 1** and **Player 2** on the **Pass & Play Setup** screen (defaults to your local profile name and `Player 2`).
- Each player starts every match with **5 hearts** and an independent elimination board.

---

## 2. Categories & Randomized 10-Card Board

- Choose one of the three primary categories:
  - **WHO** (250 Indian personalities across 10 themes)
  - **WHAT** (128 Indian foods, brands, vehicles, movies, household items, tech, snacks, and products across 8 themes)
  - **WHERE** (72 Indian cities, attractions, states, monuments, colleges, and destinations across 6 themes)
- The app automatically generates a **randomized 10-card board** mixed across subcategories and displays it on the **Board Preview** screen. You can tap **Shuffle New 10 Cards** before starting.
- Custom Boards created offline can also be played directly with manual secret selection.

---

## 3. Secret Card Assignment & Privacy

- For randomized 10-card matches, each player is automatically assigned a **distinct secret card** from the 10-card board (both players never receive the same card).
- Each player views their secret card privately one at a time, separated by a full-screen **Privacy Handoff** screen so the other player cannot peek.
- During gameplay, your own secret card is badged **`MY CARD`** on your board, cannot be eliminated, and is automatically excluded from your Final Guess candidate list.
- You can press and hold **`Hold to Peek Secret`** on the top bar anytime during your turn to remind yourself of your secret card.

---

## 4. Asking Questions & Eliminating Cards

- Players sit together and ask **unlimited verbal Yes/No questions** out loud (e.g., *"Is your card a cricketer?"*, *"Is it located in South India?"*).
- Normal questions **never cost hearts**.
- Optionally, tap the note icon in the bottom bar to log a question and Yes/No answer into **Question History**.
- Tap any opponent candidate card on your grid to **eliminate** (dim/cross out) it, or tap again to **restore** it. Eliminations are tracked privately per player and never deduct hearts.

---

## 5. Final Guessing & Five-Heart System

Each player starts with **5 hearts**:

- When ready to guess the opponent's secret card, tap **Final Guess** in the bottom bar, pick a card from the remaining opponent candidates, and confirm **Lock Final Guess**.
- **Correct Final Guess**:
  - **Immediate Victory!** The match ends right away and navigates to the **Match Results** screen revealing both secret cards and match statistics.
- **Incorrect Final Guess**:
  - **1 heart is immediately deducted** from the guessing player.
  - If the player still has hearts remaining (`> 0`), their **turn ends automatically** and switches immediately to the next active player.
  - A notification displays:
    > `"Incorrect Guess! 1 Heart Lost. It's <NextPlayer>'s Turn."`
  - The app automatically transitions to the **Privacy Handoff** screen so the phone can be passed safely.

---

## 6. Automatic Turn Switching (No "End Turn" Button)

- The manual **End Turn** button has been **completely removed** from the gameplay interface.
- Turn progression is **100% automatic**:
  - During your turn, ask as many verbal questions and eliminate/restore as many cards as you like.
  - Submitting an **incorrect final guess** automatically ends your turn, deducts 1 heart, resets the per-turn guess state, and passes control to the next active player.
  - After acknowledging the **Privacy Handoff** screen (`I Am Ready`), the next player receives their normal full turn.

---

## 7. Elimination, Game-Ending & Winning Conditions

A match ends immediately when any of the following occurs:

1. **Correct Final Guess**: The guessing player wins immediately.
2. **Zero-Heart Elimination**: If an incorrect final guess reduces a player to **0 hearts**, that player is eliminated, their turn is skipped, and instead of switching turns the match ends immediately on the **Match Results** screen with the surviving opponent declared the winner.
3. **Forfeit**: Tapping the top-left close icon and confirming **Forfeit** ends the match and awards victory to the opponent.
